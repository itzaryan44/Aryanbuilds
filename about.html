About

Hi, I'm Aryan

A ninth grader in Jharkhand who taught himself to build apps — one small, useful project at a time.

Why I build

I kept noticing small problems around me — shops with no online presence, revision that felt harder than it needed to be, apps that did too much when I just wanted something simple. Building felt like the most direct way to actually fix any of that, so I started teaching myself.

What's live right now

Project Martify connects local shops in Tier 2 and 3 Indian cities with nearby buyers. AryNotes is an offline study companion with a journal-style interface. Heyy is a messaging app with chat, calls and voice notes, built solo to learn app development end to end. All three — and whatever ships next — are on the home page.

What's next

I'm aiming to turn one of these into a real startup while I'm still in school, and I'm learning design, product, and business along the way. This site updates every time something new ships.
